# JUSTICE — ecosystem intelligence and human authority architecture

Status: **target architecture.** This is what Justice is meant to become. It is not
a description of the deployed system — for that, read
[JUSTICE-ECOSYSTEM-BASELINE.md](JUSTICE-ECOSYSTEM-BASELINE.md), which records only
what was confirmed live on 2026-11-09.

Sections 1–8 are the owner's specification, preserved as given. Section 9 is
discovery: where the deployed system already meets it, and where it contradicts it.

---

## 1. Architectural vision

JUSTICE is the Expert-in-the-Loop (EITL) authority, ecosystem intelligence, and
oversight layer within the broader AI-Nspired ecosystem.

Its purpose is to observe, understand, evaluate, and monitor the relationships
between individual systems, projects, users, and organizational ecosystems while
participating in the existing canonical Truth infrastructure.

JUSTICE is not intended to become another isolated AI platform or duplicate the
capabilities of existing systems. It will leverage the intelligence, routing,
knowledge, and infrastructure already established across the ecosystem while
maintaining its own identity, governance, authentication, and specialized
capabilities.

The objective is to establish a continuously evolving intelligence network in which
individual ecosystems can operate independently, monitor their own environments,
and contribute appropriately authorized intelligence to a larger collective system.

## 2. Core architecture

### JUSTICE — EITL and ecosystem intelligence

The overarching oversight and human-authority layer.

Responsibilities include:

- System-wide ecosystem monitoring.
- Architectural analysis and relationship mapping.
- Identity, membership, and organizational governance.
- Risk identification and anomaly detection.
- Evidence evaluation and provenance tracking.
- EITL review, recommendations, and authorization.
- Monitoring of changes across connected systems.
- Identification of inconsistencies, dependencies, and emerging patterns.

JUSTICE provides a higher-level perspective across participating ecosystems without
assuming ownership of their individual operations or data.

### Canonical intelligence infrastructure

JUSTICE participates in the existing shared intelligence architecture rather than
creating competing infrastructure.

**TRUTH — canonical knowledge and evidence**

- MASTER_TRUTHS.
- Divisional Truths.
- Provisional and evidentiary records.
- Provenance and source attribution.
- Confidence and verification classifications.

TRUTH remains the canonical knowledge infrastructure. JUSTICE does not independently
establish competing master truths.

**ORPROXY — intelligence routing**

The routing and decision layer responsible for directing intelligence requests
through the appropriate providers, models, and processing pathways.

**AI-PROXY — intelligence and analysis**

The intelligence layer responsible for prompt construction, analytical methodology,
domain reasoning, evidence evaluation, confidence assessment, and identification of
opposing interpretations and analytical gaps.

JUSTICE integrates with these components through established interfaces and
authorized bindings.

### Individual ecosystems

Participating systems retain their own operational responsibilities, identities,
data, and permissions. Examples include:

- **MIND**: personal workspace, context, and individual EITL interaction.
- **SPEAKEASY**: membership, expert participation, and organizational relationships.
- **Project systems**: development infrastructure, repositories, applications, and
  operational environments.
- **User ecosystems**: independently governed environments belonging to individual
  users, teams, or organizations.

JUSTICE provides oversight and intelligence across these environments without
requiring them to surrender operational independence.

## 3. Three levels of intelligence

### Level 1 — local intelligence

Each individual user, project, or system maintains its own operational context.

- Local knowledge and records.
- Identity and access permissions.
- Project-specific monitoring.
- Contextual analysis.
- Local risk identification.
- Independent operational decisions.

Information remains within its authorized scope.

### Level 2 — divisional intelligence

Related systems and projects contribute appropriately authorized information to
their respective divisional Truth infrastructure.

- Cross-project analysis.
- Domain-specific knowledge consolidation.
- Relationship and dependency identification.
- Shared evidence evaluation.
- Pattern recognition.
- Divisional monitoring and reporting.

Divisional intelligence preserves source attribution and the distinction between
verified information, inference, and unresolved findings.

### Level 3 — ecosystem intelligence

JUSTICE evaluates the broader ecosystem and the relationships between its
participating systems.

- Cross-divisional intelligence.
- System-wide dependency monitoring.
- Architectural consistency analysis.
- Emerging risk identification.
- Shared pattern recognition.
- Integrity and provenance monitoring.
- Identification of systemic failures and opportunities for improvement.
- EITL oversight of consequential changes.

The purpose is not to centralize every user's information. It is to develop a broader
understanding of how independently governed systems interact and affect one another.

## 4. Identity, membership, and governance

JUSTICE retains its specialized identity and organizational infrastructure.

Existing Cloudflare resources, including IDENTITY_LEDGER, GROUP_LEDGER,
CONTEXTMANAGER, and Memoria, must be investigated and mapped against the current
ecosystem before reuse or modification.

The architecture should support:

- Individual identities.
- Group and organizational identities.
- Membership and authorization levels.
- Expert participation.
- Delegated permissions.
- Scoped access to information.
- EITL authority and approval records.

Authentication, authorization, identity verification, and organizational membership
must remain distinct functions.

Credentials must never be treated as substitutes for a properly scoped authorization
model.

## 5. Architectural safeguards

The following principles are mandatory.

**A. No duplicate truth authority.** JUSTICE uses the existing TRUTH infrastructure,
including MASTER_TRUTHS and Divisional Truths. It does not create competing canonical
knowledge stores.

**B. Tenant and domain isolation.** Users and organizations cannot access another
participant's private information merely because their systems participate in the
larger ecosystem.

**C. Controlled intelligence aggregation.** Information may contribute to broader
intelligence only when its use is authorized. Provenance, ownership, classification,
and access restrictions must be preserved.

**D. Independent human authority.** AI systems may investigate, analyze, identify
risks, and recommend corrective action. They cannot silently assume human authority
or independently authorize consequential changes.

**E. Monitoring is not learning.** Identifying a pattern does not automatically make
it canonical knowledge. Promotion into shared or canonical Truth requires the
appropriate evidence, verification, and authorization process.

**F. Separation of responsibilities.** TRUTH establishes the canonical knowledge
infrastructure. ORPROXY handles routing. AI-PROXY performs intelligence and analysis.
JUSTICE provides ecosystem-level oversight and EITL authority. Individual systems
retain their own operational responsibilities.

## 6. Project intelligence and repository integration

JUSTICE will support the broader Project Intelligence initiative.

The objective is to enable an AI agent to investigate any authorized project
repository, understand its current implementation, identify its relationships to
other systems, and produce accurate, evidence-backed documentation.

The workflow should include:

1. Repository discovery and source inspection.
2. Identification of existing components and infrastructure.
3. Examination of dependencies, bindings, APIs, and deployment configuration.
4. Cross-reference against the broader ecosystem inventory.
5. Identification of architectural relationships.
6. Classification of findings as Confirmed, Inferred, or Unknown.
7. Generation and maintenance of project documentation.
8. Identification of inconsistencies, risks, and unresolved questions.
9. Submission of recommendations for EITL review.

The agent must distinguish existing implementation from proposed architecture.

It must not invent missing capabilities, overwrite established facts, expose
credentials, or modify production infrastructure without authorization.

## 7. Deployment and operational integration

JUSTICE will use the existing Cloudflare and GitHub infrastructure where appropriate.

The implementation should support:

- GitHub-based source control.
- Cloudflare Workers Builds.
- Controlled production deployments.
- Preview and validation environments.
- Independent Worker configuration.
- Existing shared Truth and intelligence interfaces.
- Runtime monitoring and observability.
- Versioned integration contracts.

Existing resource IDs, bindings, service dependencies, and actual source-code usage
must be verified before integration.

Resource names alone are insufficient to establish infrastructure identity.

Production deployment must remain subject to the established authorization policy.

## 8. Long-term objective

The ultimate objective is to establish a distributed intelligence ecosystem with
independently governed participants and a shared capacity for understanding,
monitoring, and improving the larger system.

Individual users gain intelligence and oversight capabilities within their own
environments.

Organizations gain visibility into their divisions, projects, and operational
relationships.

JUSTICE gains the ability to evaluate the larger ecosystem, identify systemic
patterns, and coordinate EITL oversight.

The result is not one centralized AI controlling multiple environments.

It is a network of independently operating systems that can share appropriately
authorized intelligence, preserve their own authority, and benefit from a
continuously improving collective understanding.

**Foundational principle.** Truth is the shared knowledge foundation. Intelligence
evaluates and interprets it. Routing connects the appropriate capabilities.
Individual ecosystems retain their operational independence. JUSTICE provides the
broader perspective and human-authority framework that holds the relationships
together.

### 9.3 Where the deployed system contradicts this architecture

1. **§2 "integrates through established interfaces and authorized bindings" — it has
   none.** The `justice` Worker has **zero service bindings**. It cannot reach
   `truth`, `orproxy` or `ai-proxy`. The direction is reversed: `ai-proxy` and
   `truth` both bind `JUSTICE` → `justice`, so the year-old oversight layer is a
   *downstream dependency of the layers it is supposed to oversee*.
2. **§5.A "does not create competing canonical knowledge stores" — the EITL path
   does exactly that.** `edit_truth` writes `truth:<id>` at confidence **100** into
   Memoria, with **no TTL**, into the namespace `truth` treats as provisional and
   expires after 24 hours (`expirationTtl: 24 * 3600`). The path is currently inert
   only because the code says `env.MEMY` and the binding is `memy`. A one-character
   fix would put permanent, maximum-confidence records into the provisional tier.
3. **§4 "credentials must never be substitutes for a scoped authorization model" —
   they are the entire model.** The EITL authority is a hardcoded constant (`MASTER_HASH`, redacted here — it is a
   live credential) compared against a KV value, and the group keys are hardcoded in
   the same bundle. There are no roles, scopes, or expiry.
4. **§5.D "cannot independently authorize consequential changes" — an anonymous
   request can.** `validate_group` writes `group:<id>:hash` to `GROUP_LEDGER` and
   returns the group key to any caller who names `group1` or `group2`; the endpoint
   answers anonymous POSTs with CORS `*`. From the code; that write path was
   deliberately not exercised.
5. **§7 "GitHub-based source control" — there is none.** Of 147 repos, none is the
   Justice source. The deployed bundle is the only copy, and its comments record
   guessed bindings (`// FIX 2: Assumed binding is JUSTICE_CHAT based on the error`).

### 9.6 Decisions required before any implementation

1. **Repair or rebuild?** The vision describes a system that does not exist. The
   deployed artifact contradicts safeguard A (competing store) and D (anonymous
   authority). Repairing the three casing/naming bugs would *activate* the §9.3
   item 2 violation.
2. **Brand transition — settled, and it supersedes the old rule.** The brand is
   moving from `AI-Nspired` / `ai-nspired.com` to `nspired.cc`. `nspired.cc` governs new
   brand architecture, new documentation and new interfaces; `AI-Nspired` and
   `ai-nspired.com` are the current legacy identity during the transition, not the
   settled name. Historical references keep their original spelling and are never
   retro-edited — including every `AI-Nspired` in this document, every `ai-nspired.com`
   URL, and the files in `docs/` that use `i.nspired.cc`.
   `AGENTS.md` rule 2 carries the same policy. The product name `ai-Ncards` is
   outside this rule: it is not renamed, and its relationship to `nspired.cc` is a
   separate product-naming decision.
3. **Where do EITL approval records live** — a new namespace, or a fourth key family
   in `identity_ledger`?
4. **Does the truth Worker gain a divisional tier** before Justice depends on one,
   given no such tier exists in its current source?
5. **Does Justice get a repository?** §7 requires source control; none exists. That
   is a GitHub write and needs explicit authorization.
6. **What is `justice`'s status while all this is decided?** It is still bound by two
   live Workers and still answers anonymous requests.
