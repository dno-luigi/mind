Engine

Off sends every answer to OpenRouter's free router and spends no daily neurons. Changing it needs the KEY; the state applies to every browser, not just this one.

Workflow

On runs the five stages — cache, decision model, browser, answer, review queue. Off sends the question straight to the model: no cache read, no decision model, no browsing, nothing queued for review. Also applies to every browser.
